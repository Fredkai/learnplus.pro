STUDY ABROAD & EXPLORE WORK IN POLAND

Your plans.
Our support.

We offer two services: support exploring work opportunities in Poland, and help applying to study in European countries or Canada.

Apply to study abroad

Planning to study in a European country or Canada? We help you explore your study options, prepare your application, and understand the next steps. Tell us your preferred country, what you want to study, your current qualifications, and where you are applying from.

Your study plan depends on your destination. We help you compare courses, application requirements, tuition fees, and living costs for the European countries or Canadian institutions you are considering.

Work opportunities in Poland

Looking for your next work opportunity in Poland? Tell us about your experience, the type of work you want to do, and whether you are already here. We help you explore suitable next steps and the support you need.
