Privacy notice

When you contact us

The contact form asks for your name, email address, selected service and message. LearnPlus uses these details to respond to your enquiry. Please avoid sending identity documents, passwords or sensitive personal details through this form.

Cloudflare processes form requests and delivers enquiries to our Gmail inbox. Google processes the resulting emails. Delivery and hosting involve these providers; their privacy terms also apply to their services.

Browser storage and request data

Saved services and your preferred list or grid view are stored in your browser on this device. You can remove them by clearing this website's browser storage. These preferences are not sent with your enquiry.

The form uses the request's IP address to limit repeated submissions. Hosting and email providers may process request information as part of operating and protecting their services.

Questions about your information

Contact info@learnplus.pro to ask about your enquiry information or request its deletion.
